JOB-SEQUENCING(jobs, n)

1. Sort jobs by decreasing profit

2. Create slot array and initialize all slots as empty

3. for each job i
4.     for j = min(deadline[i], n) - 1 down to 0
5.         if slot[j] is empty
6.             assign job i to slot[j]
7.             break

8. Print selected jobs and total profit
